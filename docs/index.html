Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026 · Last updated: September 19, 2026
The short version: Canyon Clash has no accounts and no sign-in, and we don’t collect your name, email, or contacts. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use a device advertising identifier to show and measure ads. On iOS you’re asked whether to allow this, and you can decline.

1Who this applies to

This policy covers the mobile game Canyon Clash (the “App”), published by Andrew Macon (“we”, “us”). By using the App you agree to this policy.

2Information we collect

We ourselves collect nothing. The App has no sign-up or login, and we do not ask for, or directly receive, any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • Location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data.
  • Payment information — the App has no in-app purchases.

The one exception is advertising, described next.

3Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process certain information from your device, which can include a device advertising identifier, general (non-precise) location, IP address, and ad-interaction data. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you personally.

  • On iOS, you are shown Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” the App does not use your identifier for cross-app tracking, and you’ll generally see less personalized ads.
  • Google’s handling of this data is governed by Google’s own policies: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data.

4Data stored on your device

Any gameplay data (such as your current progress, coins, or settings) is stored only on your own device by the game. It is never sent to us, and it is removed if you delete the App.

5Who we share data with

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone except Google AdMob for the advertising described in section 3. Aside from ad serving, the game does not require an internet connection and does not send your data to our own servers (we don’t operate any).

6Children’s privacy

Canyon Clash is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the App shows ads, if you are configuring it for a child-directed audience you should enable AdMob’s child-directed / family settings so that only age-appropriate, non-personalized ads are served, consistent with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU GDPR’s protections for children.

7Changes to this policy

If we change what data is collected (for example, adding analytics or in-app purchases), we will update this policy and revise the App’s privacy details on the App Store before that change takes effect. The “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
