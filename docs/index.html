1Who this applies to

This policy covers the mobile game Canyon Clash (the “App”), published by [Your name or company] (“we”, “us”). By using the App you agree to this policy.

2Information we collect

None. The App does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

Any gameplay data (such as your current progress, coins, or settings) is stored only on your own device by the game. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is removed if you delete the App.
