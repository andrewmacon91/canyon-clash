## Description  *(max 4000 chars)*
```
CANYON CLASH — run, multiply, and blast your way through an ancient desert canyon!

You command a single cyan soldier. Charge forward, slam through glowing ×2 and +25 gates to grow your one trooper into a MASSIVE army, then unleash a storm of neon lasers on the crimson horde charging straight at you.

Survive the gauntlet and you'll face the GOLDEN SCORPION — a towering armored boss that only falls to your combined firepower before exploding into a screen-filling rain of coins.

Every boss you drop, you choose an upgrade — and the canyon fights back harder. How deep can you push?

■ SIMPLE ONE-FINGER CONTROL
Just steer left and right. Pick the right gate, dodge nothing, blast everything.

■ GROW A HUGE ARMY
Chain ×2 and ×3 gates to snowball a single soldier into a battalion. Bigger crowd = more firepower.

■ ROGUELITE UPGRADES
Clear a boss, pick a power: Rapid Fire, Power Cell, Piercing Rounds, Twin Cannons, Energy Shield, or Reinforcements. Every run builds different.

■ EPIC BOSS FIGHTS
Crack the golden scorpion's armor and watch it detonate into a fortune of bouncing coins.

■ ENDLESS DIFFICULTY RAMP
Faster runs, bigger hordes, tougher enemies, and nastier bosses every level. Chase your best distance.

■ PLAYS ANYWHERE
Smooth stylized 3D that runs offline — no internet needed.

Think you can command the biggest army in the canyon? Tap to play and find out.
```
